# 12 — Human awareness study

**STATUS: NOT EXECUTED — REQUIRES THE HUMAN OPERATOR.**

Prompt §15 specifies a blinded protocol: render charts only up to the decision
timestamp, hide all future bars and outcomes, show the bot's proposed action and
uncertainty, have the reviewer record agree / veto / defer / reduce size / no opinion
plus one reason code, lock the decision, and only then reveal the outcome.

This cannot be simulated. Producing it without the owner would be exactly the
hindsight contamination the section exists to prevent, and would be a fabricated
result.

## Awareness Gap Register — scaffold only, no observations

compression · trend maturity · exhaustion · failed breakout · defended or pinned level
· news spike · liquidity gap · correlated herd move · one correlated leg held while
another runs · regime transition · obvious data anomaly.

## Prerequisites, in order

1. F-CONN-01 resolved, so decisions are being generated again to review.
2. A decision sample including **taken trades, vetoed opportunities, no-trade periods
   and random controls** — §15 names selection bias explicitly.
3. Owner time for the blinded pass.

Until (1), the sample would be empty on the demo accounts.
