## A. ASSUMPTIONS

Each is falsifiable by the artefact named.

- The strategy set is exactly the ten keys in `agent/services/strategies.js:36-47`; nothing else can enter the scan. Falsified by any scan call site passing a `compute` not resolved through `STRATEGY_REGISTRY`.
- The JS scan reaches strategies only via `pickBestSignal` (`agent/services/fib-strategy.js:440-462`), called once per timeframe at `agent/services/fib-strategy.js:522`. Falsified by any other caller of a `compute*` export.
- The C++ VPO strategy tier can place live broker orders when `VPO_ENABLED=true` and `VPO_SYMBOLS` parses, via `cpp-exec/src/vpo_dispatcher.cpp:108` → `cpp-exec/src/engine.cpp:282`. Falsified by a build flag or route that disables `ExecEngine::placeOrder` from that path.
- Bars reaching every JS strategy are produced only by `decodeTrendbars` (`agent/lib/ctrader-ws.js:408-423`). Falsified by any other bar constructor feeding `compute*`.
- `EXEC_ENGINE` and `VPO_ENABLED` are read from the process environment at the two sites cited (`agent/lib/exec-engine.js:10`, `cpp-exec/src/main.cpp:173`) and nowhere overridden at runtime. Falsified by a route that mutates `process.env`.
- Test files under `agent/services/*.test.js` are evidence of intent only (K6). Where behaviour is asserted below it is asserted from the non-test source.

## D. LAYER VERDICT

**L1.1 — settled.** Enumerated per strategy from source:
- Deterministic in `(bars, timeframe)` alone: `computeEmaPullback` (`ema-pullback.js:56`), `computeDonchianBreakout` (`donchian-breakout.js:33`), `computeRsiMeanrev` (`rsi-meanrev.js:35`), `computeVwapTrend` (`vwap-trend.js:38`), `computeVpValue` (`vp-value.js:26`), `computeRsi2` (`rsi2-reversion.js:47`), `computeFibConfluence` (`fib-confluence.js:37`). No wall-clock, no module state, no arrival ordering inside these functions.
- `computeCupHandleSignal` / `computeInvCupHandleSignal` (`cup-handle.js:311`) stamp `new Date().toISOString()` into the returned object; the predicate itself is bar-only.
- `computeFibSignal` and the scan wrapper are **not** deterministic in bars alone — F-L1-06 (wall-clock decides the closed-bar slice; `barCache` is mutable module state shared across scans; `SCAN_CONCURRENCY` is env-tunable at `fib-strategy.js:83` though result order is preserved).
- The C++ tier is arrival-ordered by construction: `recomputeLoop` (`vpo_dispatcher.cpp:60-69`) mutates atomics on a 5-second thread while `onTick` (`vpo_dispatcher.cpp:114-119`) reads them, and `ARMED→FIRED` is resolved by CAS — correct against double-fire for one strategy, and silent about which tick won.

**L1.2 — NOT EVIDENCED.** Parameter counts are source-derivable (`ema_pullback` 5: `ema-pullback.js:24-28`; `donchian_breakout` 7: `donchian-breakout.js:18-24`; `rsi_meanrev` 3 + two hardcoded RSI thresholds: `rsi-meanrev.js:11-13,52-53`; `vwap_trend` 6: `vwap-trend.js:29-34`; `vp_value` 5: `vp-value.js:16-20`; `rsi2_reversion` 8: `rsi2-reversion.js:24-34`; `fib_confluence` 7 + 4 ratios: `fib-confluence.js:26-32`; `cup_handle` 13: `vpo_strategies.cpp:124-136` mirrors). Sample size is absent from the repository — blocking token: **TIER-B**, DR-1.

**L1.3 — PARTIAL.** Regime is detectable at runtime and is detected: `regime.js:24-27` computes ADX(14) against a fixed 25 line plus ATR-expansion bands at 1.3/0.7, and `regime-gate.js:30-40` maps every strategy key to `meanrev` or `trend`. What is missing is the fitting regime: no artefact records which volatility, liquidity or session regime any strategy was fitted on, so the runtime regime cannot be compared to the fitted one. Session regime is not gated at all — `agent/loop.js:1428` scans 24/7 by explicit comment, and the four-state regime vocabulary has no session term. Blocking token for the fitted half: **TIER-B**, DR-1.

**L1.4 — settled, with one live defect.** Look-ahead is defended at the two places that matter: the forming bar is dropped before every signal evaluation (`fib-strategy.js:516`, and again for pending setups at `fib-strategy.js:764`), and `findStrictSwings` / `findSwings` use N-left/N-right pivots that cannot repaint. Donchian excludes the breakout bar from its own channel (`donchian-breakout.js:41-46`). The residual path is F-L1-06: the closed/forming boundary is decided by wall-clock against a cache entry that may be a full bar old, so which bar is "last closed" is not a property of the data. The C++ tier evaluates `microBars.back()` / `macroBars.back()` with no forming-bar exclusion (`vpo_strategies.cpp:35,77,206,451,495`); whether the Node feeder pushes closed bars only is the feeder's contract, not the sidecar's — recorded as DR-2 rather than resolved by assumption.

**L1.5 — settled. DUAL-OWNED authority statement.** Seven strategies exist on both sides: `ema_pullback`, `donchian_breakout`, `vwap_trend`, `vp_value`, `cup_handle`, `inv_cup_handle`, `fib_confluence`, plus `rsi2_reversion` (eight registrations at `cpp-exec/src/main.cpp:235-242`). `fib_618_fade` is JS-only.
- Under **`EXEC_ENGINE=js`**: `agent/services/strategies.js` is authoritative and sole. `execEngineMode()` returns `'js'` (`agent/lib/exec-engine.js:10`), `pushTrailConfig` and `backtestRemote` no-op (`exec-engine.js:93,109`), and no HTTP call reaches the sidecar, so `vpo_strategies.cpp` cannot execute.
- Under **`EXEC_ENGINE=cpp`**: authority **splits**, and this is the finding. The Node scan tier still computes JS predicates and routes its orders through `placeOrder` → `sidecar('POST','/order')` (`exec-engine.js:196-199`) — the sidecar is a transport there. Independently, when `VPO_ENABLED=true` and `VPO_SYMBOLS` parses at least one entry (`cpp-exec/src/main.cpp:173,255-259`), `vpo_strategies.cpp` becomes an **originating** decision-maker: `VpoDispatcher::tryFire` calls `engine_.placeOrder` directly (`vpo_dispatcher.cpp:108`) with no Node involvement. Two strategy tiers can then act on the same symbol from different predicates.
- Semantic identity: **not identical for any of the eight.** The C++ ports arm before confirmation where the JS requires a closed-bar confirmation (`vpo_strategies.cpp:40-43,208-212,254-259,392-395`) — an acknowledged design difference, and therefore a different predicate. On top of that: volume gate deleted (F-L1-01), timeframe floor absent (F-L1-02), stop/target quantities different (F-L1-03), POC-side condition dropped and catch radius widened 3× (F-L1-04), R:R floor absent on six of seven (F-L1-05). Extends F-L0-06 and F-L0-07.
- Two further divergences, recorded for completeness: the C++ VWAP anchor is fixed at `kDayMs` (`vpo_strategies.cpp:27`) while `anchorPeriodFor` selects week/month for 1d+ timeframes (`vwap-trend.js:22-27`) — benign only while the micro timeframe stays intraday; and every strategy is constructed with `vpoMicroTf` as its declared timeframe (`main.cpp:235-242`) even for the four that compute off `macroBars`, so the timeframe label on a VPO-originated order is wrong for `vp_value`, `cup_handle`, `inv_cup_handle` and `rsi2_reversion`.

**L2.1 — settled.** Source: cTrader `GET_TRENDBARS_RES` over WebSocket (`agent/lib/ctrader-ws.js:400-423`), decoded from delta-encoded points at a fixed 10⁻⁵ scale (`POINTS_PER_PRICE = 100_000`, `ctrader-ws.js:403`, explicitly *not* per-symbol digits). Granularity: the `TRENDBAR_PERIODS` ladder from `1m` up (`ctrader-ws.js:77`), plus operator-supplied custom timeframes admitted at `agent/loop.js:1463`.
