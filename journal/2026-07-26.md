## Two things that were wrong and are now corrected in the repo

1. **The "Agent unreachable" toast asserted a cause it could not know.**
   `fetch()` rejects identically for an agent that is down, DNS gone, TLS
   refused, a phone dropping Wi-Fi mid-request, and a reply that never arrived
   because the agent was busy. The copy now says only what is known, and
   distinguishes idempotent reads ("nothing was changed") from order actions
   ("this may or may not have been carried out — check the position before

### The defects worth remembering

- **`SUM` skips NULLs.** Both daily-loss caps summed `net_pnl` over today's
  closed trades, and three closure paths leave that column NULL — including
  the broker-side close, which is the normal stop-out exit. A day of
  stop-outs summed to zero and neither cap tripped. The brake was off for
  exactly the trades most likely to be losses.
- **`Array.prototype.filter` hands the callback the ORIGINAL array.** The
  cockpit's tape de-overlap filter compared each tick against the pre-filter
  neighbour, so it never dropped anything. Reading `arr[i-1]` inside a filter
  as "the previous KEPT item" is the trap; it is the previous CANDIDATE.
- **A guard can protect against the case it disabled.** The order dedupe read
  `trades`; the ambiguous-submission path writes no `trades` row. It covered
  the retry it had turned off and missed the one that could still double.
