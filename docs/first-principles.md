# First principles — the two owner-stated sets, in one place

Two sets of principles govern this repo. The 07-09-2026 set describes the
TRADING SYSTEM (what edge is being harvested and at what horizon); the
11-09-2026 set describes the MACHINE (how accounts, switches, records and
vetoes behave). Both are owner-confirmed; both are quoted here in substance
so that a fresh session or Codex can test the code against them without this
conversation. `docs/first-principles-audit-2026-09-19.md` is the measured
audit against both, with the six-wave plan that brought the code under them
and its execution log; `docs/owner-principles-plan-2026-09-11.md` is the
earlier build plan for the 11-09 set. CLAUDE.md carries the 11-09 set as
standing rules and the P7 scope.

## A. The trading system (owner, 07-09-2026 17:50 SGT)

Where the evidence sat when these were stated (№ 7,379): the momentum edge
on the book's symbols is real at 10–60 trading days (trail rule avgR +0.15 to
+0.61, PF 1.5–2.9) and absent at ≤ 96 h; the non-momentum stack (12
strategies, intraday-to-daily, capped exits) measured PF 0.37 / −0.17R over
88 clean closes.

1. **Horizon is the design variable.** Every rule states its horizon; the
   momentum system's is weeks. Book decisions on the daily close only; weekly
   loss accounting for the momentum family, not daily.
2. **Exit asymmetry sets expectancy, not entry accuracy.** No win-rate goal
   for the momentum family; the goal is profit factor and tail share (closes
   beyond +2R).
3. **Size by volatility target at portfolio level.** Weight = target vol /
   asset vol (ATR-based), margin-aware; the universe pre-filtered by min-lot
   affordability per account, so refusals happen at universe build.
4. **Breadth is the fuel.** An explicit momentum universe as data (indices,
   FX majors, commodities, crypto, mega-caps) with per-account tradability.
5. **One system per horizon, one account per system.** Guards built for
   hours never touch the weeks-horizon book. (Reconciled with B·9 below.)
6. **Judge at the horizon's cadence.** A daily mark-to-market equity curve
   per account and a pre-registered checkpoint (PF, tail share, max
   drawdown) on a date, instead of "30 closes".
7. **Costs and gaps are in the rule.** Minimum hold and a re-entry throttle
   after a forced exit; gap risk handled by vol sizing, not by sweeps.
8. **The machine reports itself.** A daily report generated by the agent and
   posted on schedule; the chat session is not the reporting path.

## B. The machine (owner, 11-09-2026 ~20:10 SGT; the standing text is in CLAUDE.md)

1. **No demo/live distinction.** An account is only "how much is inside
   it"; only routing may read `is_live`; every policy gate reads balance and
   evidence.
2. **Prioritise for opportunities.** The tick-based switch is on/off per
   account by a human AND automatically by the bot.
3. **Codebase-built blockages are addressed, not carried.**
4. **"Unknown" must not happen** after four weeks of trading; every trade
   has a reason.
5. **The `.md` plans are checked** — kept audited against the code.
6. **The website shows no fake result.** UI switches are logic-built.
7. **Vetoes are part of what is minimised.** (The position cap stays as is.)
8. **Trade direction is key** for trending / momentum trading. (Shorts on
   the momentum book under the 9/10 conviction floor.)
9. **No restricted trading for certain accounts.** Setups are for all
   accounts and not hardcoded.

## C. Where the two sets meet: A·5 against B·9

A·5 ("one account per system") and B·9 ("setups are for all accounts")
read as opposites and are not. The reconciliation, in one sentence:

> Every account may run every system it can fund; a system on trial runs on
> one account until its checkpoint.

So B·9 governs eligibility (nothing is hard-coded to an account id; the
momentum pass, the tick switch and the arming bar are open to every funded
account on the same evidence) and A·5 governs evidence (a system whose live
record has not yet earned it runs on ONE account, at the verdict's pending
scale, until the dated checkpoint — today `tsmom_long` on …0058 to
2026-12-19, declared in `agent/config/strategy-pins.json` `_trial`). A trial
that passes its checkpoint widens under B·9; one that fails is switched off
under A·2's evidence, not carried.
