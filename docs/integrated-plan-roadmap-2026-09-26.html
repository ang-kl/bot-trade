Standing rules, risks & hard constraints

Every wave

Full CLAUDE.md gate with a fresh TMPDIR. Every mutation check confirmed applied with grep -c before/after. Read-back after every deploy: /health, the item's own route, /state/heartbeats, all positions protected.

The trace rule

Every PR touching src/ or a website-read route carries a before/after trace table. No traces inside a graded P1/P4 window; NEW-1 lands first so traces don't count as visible tabs.

Hard constraints

No change to agent/config/tick-validation.json or its pins; maxOpenPositions 5 / book 8; mandatory TP1; owner principles 1–9. Nothing under cpp-exec/**, cpp-*/** without explicit item scope.

No fake results

Never delete records; never hide a record to make a number look good (principle 6). Every UI state carries a word/label, never colour alone.
