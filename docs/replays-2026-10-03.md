## What this means for 10 October

1. None of the four changes the live record for the better. The stop width is unproven,
   the direction filter is unmeasurable, the R:R gate should not be lowered, and the
   pooled verdict only confirms that the three strategies with enough closes lose.
2. The sides flip with the window. In the last 30 days shorts won and longs lost (SELL PF
   1.94, BUY 0.47); over the 90-day evidence window the pooled donchian and rsi2 losses are
   on the SELL side (PF 0.46 and 0.42). A rule built on one window's side would have been
   wrong in the other. Direction is not a stable edge in this data.
3. What the data does support for a single live account: trade only what measured at or
   above PF 1 with a positive expectancy lower bound, and nothing measured that. The
   nearest cells are …0949's bar (PF 1.01) and pre-open (PF 1.15) bases, both with a
   negative lower bound. The honest statement is that no strategy in the book has a
   demonstrated edge at the 30-trade bar, and the 10 Oct account should be sized for
   evidence gathering, not for return.
4. Five record defects were found by the replays and are listed below; the first and the
   second change what the dashboards say today and should be fixed before any further
   measurement is trusted.

## D1 — initial stop width

**Population.** 1,690 rows from `GET /state/trades?account=all&limit=1000` (offsets 0 and
1000) → 1,363 closed → 293 closed in the 30 days before 12:50Z → 287 joined to a
postmortem (`GET /state/postmortems?account=<id>&limit=100`, five accounts; 6 missing: 4
pending on the route, 2 with net_pnl 0) → 280 replay inputs after excluding 5 trades with
no stop recorded and 2 longs whose "initial" stop sat above entry.

**Method.** stop0 = `broker_sl_initial` else `sl_price` (the same rule as `realised_rr`);
the stop at k × the original distance for k = 1, 1.5, 2 with the recorded target; the
repository's own `lib/exit-replay.js` `replayExit` with rule `no_cap`; R in the wider-risk
unit (constant R per trade, so size would be 1/k). Truncated windows counted honestly and
separately at mark-to-window R. Costs not charged (`spread_at_entry` on 25 rows);
measured commission plus swap is a median 0.02 R, p90 0.14 R per trade.

**Calibration.** Of 82 recorded exits at or through the original stop, the replay stops
74 (90.2%), 8 truncated, 0 ambiguous; median |replay R − recorded R| 0.047 on the 96
resolved. 84 recorded "stop" exits were at a better price than the original stop (a
trailed or moved stop fired); the no-trail replay holds through those by design.

| Width | Resolved (stop / target) | Truncated | Excl. truncated: win, PF, ΣR | Incl. at window mark: win, PF, ΣR, mean R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1× | 96 (90 / 6) | 184 (65.7%) | 6.3%, 0.163, −75.4 | 41.8%, 0.749, −28.2, −0.101 |
| 1.5× | 55 (48 / 7) | 225 (80.4%) | 12.7%, 0.264, −35.3 | 42.5%, 0.699, −25.5, −0.091 |
| 2× | 42 (34 / 8) | 238 (85.0%) | 19.0%, 0.302, −23.7 | 43.9%, 0.676, −21.7, −0.077 |

The 90 trades the replay stops at the original stop (Σ −90 R): at 1.5× still stopped 48,
target 1, unresolved 41 (40 of 41 below entry at the window end, mean mark −0.53 R); at 2×
stopped 34, target 2, unresolved 54 (50 of 54 below entry). By side, SELL is the only
positive group on any reading (PF 1.46 at 1×, 1.34 at 2×, n 137); BUY is negative on every
width (0.39 / 0.39 / 0.37, n 143). No strategy cell resolves more than 19 trades at 1× or
14 at 1.5×.

**Bound direction.** "Excluding truncated" is biased toward stop-outs (understates every
width, the wider ones more); "including at mark" is biased upward for the wider stops
because the unresolved trades are underwater with the stop as the likely next event. The
true value sits between, nearer the pessimistic end for 1.5× and 2×. 126 of 280 windows end
at or before the recorded close and cannot resolve any width; bars after entry are a median
6 (p25 3). Not modelled: 62 manual, book and rank exits, 32 time-cap exits, 54 trades with
no target, intrabar order.

## D2 — direction filter

**Population.** The same 293 closed trades (BUY 149, SELL 144). Risk events from
`GET /state/risk-events?account=all&symbol=<s>&limit=500` (64 symbols; 36 at the 500-row
cap, no offset on the route) plus 10 per-account pulls: joined by `risk_event_id` 262, by
account+symbol+side+time 14, none 17.

**Classification.** `proposal_json.trend_at_evaluation` present on 84 of 276 joined events
and carrying a direction on 7: six agreed with the trend and lost together (ΣR −4.36, 2 of
6 won); one disagreed and lost (US2000 BUY vp_value, −1.03 R). 286 are unknown: 192 with no
field (closed_market_limit 94, auto_signal 56 of which 50 pre-11-09, htf_limit 28,
momentum_book 8, pending 6), 77 `ranging`, 17 no risk event. The BUY/SELL asymmetry lives
inside "unknown" (BUY unknown PF 0.47 n 146; SELL unknown PF 1.95 n 140).

**What the policy does today.** The scan path's `checkRegimeGate` (`regime-gate.js:131-170`)
blocks mean-reversion in volatile and trending-against regimes and trend strategies in quiet
and trending-against regimes; `ranging` passes both sides; unknown or stale fails open
(`regime_gate {on:true, maxRegimeAgeMin:240, blockOnStaleRegime:false}`).
`direction-policy.js:58` (momentum book) requires trend-down for shorts and no trend
condition for longs; `direction-policy.js:83` (tick permits) maps up→BUY, down→SELL,
unknown→both. The long half of the proposed filter is therefore a new rule on every path; the
short half is a tightening on the scan and tick paths. Regime definition: ADX(14) ≥ 25 →
trending with direction by DI; the regime bars are whichever of 1d/4h/1h is cached with 40
bars, else 1h × 80; the timeframe is not recorded on the row; regimes are pruned at 30 days
and only the latest per symbol is exposed, so the 286 unknowns cannot be back-filled.

## D3 — the R:R gate with costs

**Unit defect (verified in code and on 50 of 50 sample rows).** `refusal_scores.sl` is the
proposal's pre-floor stop; the gate's `rr` in `bad_rr 1.38<3` is computed after
`risk.js:2226-2237` widens the stop to the hourly-ATR floor (`checks.stop_floor.to`). 354 of
357 `bad_rr` vetoes in the 500-row window were floored, median 4.1×. `r_reached` therefore
describes a trade about 4× tighter than any the gate would have sized: the sample's 11 target
hits are 55.5 R in ledger units and 13.4 R in sent-stop units; its 29 stop-outs do not
translate without bars.

**Thresholds on the gate's own rr** (500-row window, 357 bad_rr rows, 143 distinct setups,
30-09 to 02-10): 2.0 admits 27 rows / 13 setups; 2.5 admits 11 / 5; 3.0 admits 0. On the
pre-floor rr the same rows read ≥ 3.0 for 243 of 357 (68%): the floor, not the target, makes
them bad_rr. Per strategy at 2.0 (distinct setups): donchian 8 of 21, va_breakout 2 of 11,
vp_value 3 of 63, rsi_meanrev 0 of 33, vwap_trend 0 of 8, fib_618_fade 0 of 7.

**Costs on the exposed sample** (40 scored rows; the routes expose only the newest 50 rows,
one 12-hour slice of 01-10, 5.9% of the 7-day population): the paid share is unchanged at
11 of 40 rows (3 of 14 distinct setups) under every cost model, because the winners are 3–8 R
targets at the tight stop; mean R per row 0.66 → 0.30 (schedule) → 0.22 (plus measured
spread); per distinct setup +0.26 → −0.19 → −0.29. Mean schedule cost 0.34 R per row; FX rows
are below cost (USDHKD 4.4 R per round trip). Per strategy: rsi_meanrev (n 5) and donchian
(n 7) stay positive after every cost; vp_value (n 31, 25 stops) goes from +0.28 to −0.15 /
−0.24.

**Real-money comparison** (taken trades, planned rr from stored sl and tp): last 30 days,
planned 2.0–3.0 n 41, PF 0.26, net −1,700.84 (14 vwap_trend at −1,480), mean −0.07 R;
planned ≥ 3.0 n 97, PF 1.05, +122.93, mean −0.10 R. All 954 closes: 2–3 PF 0.375 (n 97)
against ≥ 3 PF 0.63 (n 244). Trade-plans with `planned_r` (32 rows): 2–3 n 10 mean +0.35 R
against ≥ 3 n 15 +0.05 R, small-n, the other way.

**Measured costs** (293 closes): commission bps of notional round trip, median: stock_us
2.31, stock_hk 29.8, commodity/fx/index/crypto 0; swap bps per day: index 0.94, crypto 2.5;
spread bps: stock_us 2.8 (n 18), crypto 18.8 (n 4).

**Population answer** needs a row-level route over `refusal_scores` joined to
`checks_json.rr` and `stop_floor.to`, or a scorer change that replays at the floored stop
with costs and stores `rr_gate` beside `r_reached`. Not built.
