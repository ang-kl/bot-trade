bot-trade Architecture

Six processes on Railway, one broker. The Node service decides; two C++ gateways place and protect; two C++ scanners mirror the feed; one C++ verifier reads the broker on its own session and judges the rest. This page shows how work flows, how fast each part runs, and the logic that lets a trade in and takes it out. Every number is from the code, with the file named.

1. The services and who talks to whom

Node (JavaScript)C++ sidecarsoutside the repothe money path

One rule shapes the whole map: only the two gateways can place, amend or close. Node never holds order authority itself; it sends a request with a one-use permit and reads back what the broker holds. The verifier can read the broker but has no write route at all, which is what makes its verdicts independent.

2. The clocks: how fast each part runs

Three speeds coexist. Decisions are batched (about a minute). Protection is fast and narrow (seconds). The broker-side stop is instant, because the broker holds it even if every service here is down.

3. Entry logic: what a signal has to pass to become a trade

The order below is the order in the code. A refusal at any step is recorded with its reason (owner principle 4: every trade, and every refusal, has a reason).

  1. A producer proposes. Strategy autopilot on the scan, the momentum account's daily pass, the cross-sectional book, the tick firer on the gateway, or a manual route. The inventory of producers is pinned in agent/lib/entry-producers.js.
  2. Entry-mode admission. Each account is TIME_BASED (bars), TICK_MOMENTUM or STOPPED. admitEntry is asked at the start of the trade step and again inside placeOrder, so nothing bypasses it (entry-mode.js:749).
  3. Vetoes, minimised. Regime gate (no fading a trend), direction policy (shorts on the book only above the 9/10 conviction floor), the R:R floor (3.0 for momentum), a 20-minute dedupe window, cluster conviction. Vetoes are counted as a goal to shrink (principle 7).
  4. Account pre-gate. Balance in USD through the FX rate table (SGD accounts convert via USDSGD; a stale rate over 26 h refuses), the daily-loss stop, the shared margin pool, the fundable universe, and the caps: 5 open positions, 8 on the book (account-pregate.js:127; risk.js:371).
  5. Sizing. Per-trade risk 0.25–3 % of balance from Tune; momentum accounts size to a 10 % volatility target; lots rounded to the symbol's step (risk.js:1685; lot-sizing.js:57).
  6. Permit. The entry-intent ledger reserves one permit for this intent and the order redeems it; a second send with the same intent is refused (entry-ledger.js:246,315).
  7. Gateway guard and pacing. OrderGuard checks halt, volume cap and bracket; the pacer holds 40 requests a second with a 25 % reserve for protection calls; then the broker (cpp-exec/src/order_guard.cpp, request_pacer.cpp).
  8. Stop policy on the fill. Every stop is sent with trigger Opposite; broker-side trailing is requested once the stop locks profit (never on book rows); the controller stamps existing positions one per account per pass after a canary (stop-policy.js:37-127; stop-policy-controller.js:169).

4. Exit and protection: the stack that watches a position

Each layer has one authority and a named owner. Where two layers could move the same stop, the code gives the position to one of them (the one-horizon rule) rather than letting both act.

Broker-side stop always on

Opposite trigger on every stop; trailing at the broker once in profit. Survives every outage here. exec-engine.js:1146

Fast monitor ladder

Peak trail is the only exit-timing rule on managed accounts; +1R partial take for mean reversion only; the time cap applies to intraday rows, weeks-horizon rows are exempt. managed-exit.js:47-85

Profit keeper + TrailEngine

Since-entry 3×ATR(22) trail spec pushed to the gateway, which ratchets on every tick. Managed accounts and book rows are fenced out. profit-keeper.js:790

Momentum book

3×ATR(20) stop and trail, 8 positions per account, rank exit on the daily pass after a 24 h minimum hold, partial TP1 overlay. momentum-book.js:146-177

Never-loosen rail

Every MOVE_SL asks the sidecar to read the broker's stop first; a looser level is refused and the stored stop becomes what the broker holds. engine.cpp:883

Closed-market hold

An exit refused with MARKET_CLOSED is held until the symbol's session opens instead of resent every minute. closed-market-hold.js:44

Account circuit breakers

Equity stop flattens and disarms one account on its daily drawdown; the adaptive breaker disarms a strategy on a losing streak; the performance breaker alerts on a bad rolling profit factor and does nothing else auto-disarm removed 03-10. equity-stop.js, adaptive-breaker.js, performance-breaker.js

Reconciler

Adopts broker positions the bot did not open, refuses another account's reply, voids duplicate rows, and treats a broker-trailed tightening as its own work rather than tampering. reconciler.js:328-907

Protection audit

Every 60 s, 4 s per account: no naked position, every target present. naked-position-guard.js:384

Money record

Closed trades get realised P&L from the broker's deal history; a verdict about an earlier false close is superseded when the row closes again later rule added 03-10. pnl-backfill.js:162

Independent verdicts

cpp-verify reads every account's positions on its own session and writes verdicts and a protection watch; Node shows them beside its own view. cpp-verify/src/main.cpp:300

MAE / Chandelier observer removed 03-10

Never produced a usable reading in production and was a third stop authority. Only its ATR helper and the keeper's since-entry trail spec remain.

6. The two accounts of truth

Everything above reduces to one habit: a reading from the service that acts is never trusted alone. The gateway holds the broker session and reports what it sent; the verifier reads the broker on another session and reports what is held; the heartbeats show whether each controller ran, not whether it was configured. When two readings of one subsystem disagree, the one that updated most recently is the one to believe, and the disagreement itself is the finding.
